Data & Sovereignty

Your family's record stays yours.

Hestia keeps your family's evidence in your own Google account — not on an external server, not shared with your child's school or anyone else, unless you decide to share it.

Your account. Your record.

Everything Hestia captures lives in a space tied to your own Google account, not a shared database.

Zero central extraction.

No child data sold, profiled, or handed to a third party. Nothing leaves without your explicit action.

You decide who sees it.

If a record needs to go to a school, a professional, or a review meeting — that's always your deliberate choice.

Built for home, not institutions.

Hestia doesn't ask you to change how your family already works. It sits alongside your day.

Built-in Safeguarding

Your judgement. Your record. Your control.

When something worth flagging happens, that record belongs to you — the parent or carer who noticed it. Hestia gives you a dedicated safeguarding space, kept separate from day-to-day notes, ready if and when you need to share it.

No one else can access your safeguarding record without your explicit action. It exists to protect — and you hold the key.
