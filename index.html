The Arena Ecosystem

One underlying philosophy.
Different tools for different people.

Learning does not happen in one place, and the people supporting a child rarely work from the same system. Arena is being built as a connected ecosystem of sovereign tools — each designed for a particular context, while sharing a common approach to evidence, continuity, human judgement and data sovereignty.

Technology

HIVEMIND

One AI Teacher Engine. Different contexts.

HIVEMIND is Arena's shared AI Teacher Engine. Rather than building a separate AI system for every product, Arena uses one governed intelligence layer that can work across different contexts while adapting its language, available information and purpose to the person using it. A practitioner should receive professional, structured support. A parent should receive clear, practical language appropriate to their child and their situation. The underlying intelligence is shared. The context is not.

  • Shared cross-product AI engine
  • Context-sensitive responses
  • Product-specific language and tone
  • Governed access to information
  • Human practitioner/parent remains responsible for decisions
  • Designed as assistance, not automated authority

Armoury

The knowledge infrastructure beneath Arena.

Armoury is the structured knowledge and taxonomy infrastructure that allows Arena to work with educational frameworks, qualifications, specifications and other source material as structured knowledge rather than simply treating documents as files.

  • Educational taxonomy
  • Curriculum and qualification structures
  • Regional educational frameworks
  • Structured source knowledge
  • Evidence-to-outcome relationships
  • Maintained source infrastructure
  • Foundation for intelligent matching and interpretation

Arena is not simply storing evidence. It is building the knowledge infrastructure required to understand what that evidence means.

Technology

SECO

Share what is needed. Nothing more.

SECO provides the controlled evidence packaging and exchange layer for Arena. It allows authorised evidence to be prepared for sharing with another organisation while preserving the boundaries around the original record.

  • Controlled evidence export
  • Structured evidence packages
  • Permission-based sharing
  • Context preservation
  • Data minimisation
  • School/practitioner controlled exchange
  • No requirement for the receiving organisation to access the source environment

Current destinations: Local Authority · Police · Subject Access Request · Social Worker · Awarding Body · Mainstream School.

The Arena Model

Different people. Different contexts. One continuity.

A parent may see a child's learning differently from a tutor. A tutor may hold evidence that a school has never seen. A support professional may understand a need that is invisible in a conventional school record. An external organisation may contribute meaningful learning without belonging to the school's system.

Arena is designed to allow those contexts to remain distinct while making meaningful continuity possible.

Connection does not require centralisation.

Sharing is controlled, authorised and user-requested. Data is only ever pushed — a user must actively choose to send a specific piece of evidence, and choose who receives it. There is no automatic background synchronisation between environments.

The Sovereign Principle

Your data. Your context. Your control.

Arena is built on a simple principle: the people and organisations responsible for a child's learning should not have to surrender ownership of that information in order to use better tools. Arena's sovereign architecture keeps identifiable family, learner and practitioner data within the relevant local environment rather than creating a centralised student database.

  • Sovereign data ownership
  • Local data custody
  • No central student database
  • Human-controlled sharing
  • No background synchronisation
  • Additive evidence records
  • Exit portability
  • AI assists; people remain responsible
